Curious?

What can Thinklabs help us with?

We bring strategy, brand identity, film and photography, digital marketing, web development, signage, and event production together under one roof. We can build from the ground up or join you for a specific project.

Where is your studio based?

You'll find us at Lunà Building, Gorordo Avenue, Cebu City, Philippines ↗. We collaborate with teams near and far.

What does working together look like?

We start with a conversation about your goals, audience, timeline, and budget. From there, we put together a tailored scope and a clear plan for the project.

How long does a project take?

Every project is different. Once we understand the scope, we'll agree on a realistic timeline together before the work begins.

How do we get started?

Email info@thinklabstudio.com or call +63 976 412 6752. Tell us a little about what you have in mind. We'll take it from there.
